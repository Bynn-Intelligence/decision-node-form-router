# Support rules for every message

- A workspace locked or suspended because a payment failed goes to Billing, not Account access: only Billing can lift a payment suspension. It is High urgency when people cannot work, Critical when a whole team cannot sign in.
- A charge taken twice, or a wrong amount, goes to Billing.
- A message that mentions a data breach, a stolen password or someone else using the account goes to Account access, Critical.
- When a message says only "see attached", route by what the screenshot shows.
