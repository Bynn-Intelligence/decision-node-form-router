# Claim rules for every claim

- Belongings stolen from a car go to Theft, which settles the broken window in the same claim. Motor claims takes car damage only when nothing was stolen.
- Water still coming in, or a home that cannot be locked or lived in, is Critical.
- A car that cannot be driven safely is High.
- A photo that does not show the damage described is flagged for a person to review. The router never rejects a claim.
