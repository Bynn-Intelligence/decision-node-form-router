# Dispute rules for every dispute

- When a merchant charged the same purchase twice and a refund is also missing, it is a Billing error: the billing team claims both back in one case.
- Several online charges the customer did not make, within minutes, are Fraud and Critical: block the card first.
- Likely fraud is a flag for the fraud team to review. The router never rejects a dispute.
- A refund less than 10 business days late is Normal.
