# Trust rules for every report

- Any seller who asks for payment outside Swapfield, by gift card, bank transfer or crypto, goes to Scams first, whatever the item. Scams is Critical while the listing is live.
- Live animals are prohibited items, but a listing that also asks for payment outside Swapfield goes to Scams.
- A report that names no problem with the listing, or only insults the seller, is flagged as possible bad faith for review, never dismissed.
