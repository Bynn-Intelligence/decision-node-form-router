# Repair rules for every request

- Water coming through or near a light fitting, a socket or a fuse box goes to Electrical first, Critical: the electrician makes it safe, then books the plumber.
- No heating or no hot water between October and April is High; with a baby, an elderly or an unwell tenant it is Critical.
- A leak that is contained, such as a drip into a bowl, is Normal.
- Pests seen once are Normal; pests in food or bedding are High.
