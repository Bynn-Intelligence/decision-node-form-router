# Document rules for every submission

- Keystead accepts a passport, a national ID card or a residence permit for opening an account. A driving licence is not accepted: route it to Manual review, where a person asks for another document.
- A document whose type cannot be told from the photo goes to Manual review.
- A name that differs only by a common short form, such as Robbie for Robert, is marked for review, never refused.
- The router only sorts submissions. A person makes every decision about an account.
