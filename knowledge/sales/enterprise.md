# Enterprise sales

## What we own
Organisations of more than 200 people, and any team that needs single sign-on, a data processing agreement, a security questionnaire, purchase-order invoicing, data residency or a custom contract.

## Examples
- A logistics company moving 1,400 dispatchers before a contract ends.
- A hospital group asking for single sign-on and an audit log.

## Not ours
- Teams of up to 200 people on standard terms: Small business sales.
- Agencies and resellers: Partnerships.
