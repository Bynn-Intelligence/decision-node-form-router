# Small business sales

## What we own
Teams of up to 200 people asking about plans, prices, a trial, a demo or how to get started, on standard terms paid by card.

## Examples
- A cleaning company with 12 staff asking which plan includes route planning.
- A plumber asking for a trial before the busy season.

## Not ours
- Anything needing single sign-on, a custom contract or invoicing by purchase order: Enterprise sales.
- Agencies, consultants and resellers, even small ones: Partnerships.
