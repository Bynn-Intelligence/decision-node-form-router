# Sales rules for every lead

- Agencies, consultants and resellers go to Partnerships, whatever their size, also when they ask for a plan for their own team. Their partner manager sells them their own plan too.
- A company of more than 200 people, or any request for single sign-on, a security review or a custom contract, goes to Enterprise sales.
- A lead whose current contract with another vendor ends within 30 days is High urgency. A purchase decision this week is Critical.
- A message with no company and no question beyond a word or two goes to Sales triage, Low urgency.
