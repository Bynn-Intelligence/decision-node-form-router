# Partnerships

## What we own
Agencies, consultants, resellers, referral partners and software vendors who want to build an integration. One partner manager owns the whole relationship, including the partner's own subscription.

## Examples
- A digital agency that wants a plan for its own staff and to offer Quillmere to its clients.
- An accounting firm that wants to refer its customers.
- A software company asking for API access to build a connector.

## Not ours
- Companies buying only for their own staff: Small business or Enterprise sales.
