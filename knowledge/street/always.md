# Street rules for every report

- Anything that has fallen across a road, cycle lane or pavement goes to Roads, which clears it first, whatever fell: a branch, a sign or a streetlight. Parks and trees takes trees still standing and things inside parks.
- A hazard in a cycle lane or a road is High; one that has already made people swerve or fall is Critical.
- Graffiti that is offensive or on a school is Normal; other graffiti is Low.
- A report about private property is passed to City triage.
